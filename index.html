Do plankton make sounds?

Almost none do. What you hear here are real ocean sounds, recreated: a diver’s breathing, waves, snapping shrimp, ship engines, and sonar. Musical tones only play when sonification is on, and those tones are data turned into sound.

Not to scale: plankton are shown thousands of times larger than life, and a real jellyfish is about a thousand times bigger than a diatom.
