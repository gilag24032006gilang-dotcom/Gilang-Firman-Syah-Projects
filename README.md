# Micro Ocean Dive

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](LICENSE)

*A bedtime story · An immersive dive*

Shrink down to the size of plankton. Drift with glass-shelled diatoms by day,
then swim through living light at night.

## ▶️ Play
**Live version:** https://gilag24032006gilang.github.io/micro-ocean-dive/

Or download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser.

## ✨ Features
- **Dad's story mode**: a narrated journey told in three acts
- **Free dive**: explore at your own pace
- **Creatures you'll meet**: diatoms, copepods, jellyfish, comb jellies and dinoflagellates
- **Ocean sound design**: recreated real ocean sounds (a diver's breathing, waves,
  snapping shrimp, ship engines, sonar), with optional sonification that turns data into sound
- Best experienced with headphones 🎧

## 🌊 Did you know?
- Phytoplankton produce about **50%** of Earth's oxygen.
- Oceans have become about **30%** more acidic since the industrial era.
- A single dinoflagellate flash lasts only about **0.1 seconds**.
